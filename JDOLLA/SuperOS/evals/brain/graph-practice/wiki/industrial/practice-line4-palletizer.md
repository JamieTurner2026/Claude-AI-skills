# Line 4 palletizer
Practice note. The line 4 palletizer stacks cartons from the case packer onto pallets. The palletizer runs a four layer pattern and the palletizer cell has a light curtain.

## Status
The line 4 palletizer cycle is stable since the gripper swap. Open item: pallet dispenser jams once a shift.
