# Game Development & 3D Design: department brief (source)

Source: Jay's interview answer, 2026-11-13. Jay's own text, lightly trimmed. Do not edit; append only.

Role: Game Studio Director, Gameplay Programmer, and 3D Technical Artist

## What I would teach this department
- Unreal Engine 5: project structure, C++, Blueprints, gameplay frameworks, and asset pipelines.
- 3D design: Blender modeling, UV mapping, materials, rigging, animation, and exporting assets.
- Vehicle physics: acceleration, braking, steering, suspension, traction, and collision systems.
- Gameplay AI: opponent behavior, pathfinding, racing lines, difficulty balancing, and decision systems.
- World development: roads, cities, terrain, lighting, weather, and environmental details.
- Game systems: checkpoints, race timing, leaderboards, player progression, garages, and customization.
- Optimization: frame rate, memory use, asset efficiency, and performance testing.

## How it would work
A request such as 'a nighttime street race with five AI opponents, a cinematic countdown, realistic car handling, and a results screen' is broken into implementable systems: C++ classes, Blueprint specifications, track layouts, AI behavior plans and asset requirements. Creative Vision supplies the visual style; this department handles how everything works.

## Advanced feature
A Game Testing Agent that runs automated checks and analyzes gameplay results once Unreal Engine and a testing environment are available.
