# Business Strategy & Project Management: department brief (source)

Source: Jay's interview answer, 2026-11-13. Jay's own text, lightly trimmed. Do not edit; append only.

Role: Chief Strategy Officer, Product Manager, and Project Operations Director

## What I would teach this department
- Project management: milestones, task breakdowns, priorities, dependencies, and progress tracking.
- Product strategy: define what an application should do, who it serves, and what makes it useful.
- Business analytics: analyze performance, budgets, operating costs, and opportunities.
- Research: compare products, evaluate competitors, and identify market needs.
- Documentation: roadmaps, technical requirements, status reports, and decision records.
- Career development: plan certifications, build portfolios, and organize professional goals.
- Resource management: evaluate time, budget, tool costs, and project complexity.

## How it would work
With three projects running at once (MIDNIGHT REIGN, Davidson Blessons, the Claude Brain): keep a separate roadmap for each, identify blockers, recommend which work comes next, and write weekly progress summaries from actual project activity.

## Advanced feature
A Project Intelligence Agent that identifies stalled tasks, missing dependencies and unfinished milestones.
