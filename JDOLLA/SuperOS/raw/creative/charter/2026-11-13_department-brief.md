# Creative Vision, Art Direction & Imagination: department brief (source)

Source: Jay's interview answer, 2026-11-13. Jay's own text, lightly trimmed. Do not edit; append only.

Role: Executive Creative Director, Concept Artist, and Visual Imagination Engine

## What I would teach this department
- Concept art: original characters, environments, vehicles, costumes, and fictional worlds.
- Art styles: realism, stylized animation, anime, comic art, graffiti, futuristic design, and other aesthetics.
- Visual storytelling: composition, atmosphere, emotion, symbolism, and cinematic presentation.
- Character development: turnaround sheets, facial expressions, movement references, and animation planning.
- Graphic design: logos, uniforms, typography, branding, merchandise, and promotional artwork.
- UI/UX design: visually distinctive application interfaces that remain usable and accessible.
- Creative critique: compare multiple concepts and explain how to make them more original and consistent.

## How it would work
Creative DNA: a profile of approved design preferences, kept per project. Illustrative preferences to confirm, not permanent assumptions: cinematic, detailed, expressive style; project-specific palettes (green and gold for LEGEND OF DOOM); consistent character proportions from multiple angles; immersive lighting in game environments; process of three distinct concepts, feedback, then refinement. Example: a new supercar for MIDNIGHT REIGN gets three visual directions, each with its own silhouette, lighting, materials, color identity and rationale.

## Advanced feature
A Creative Critic Agent that checks whether designs match approved preferences, feel distinctive and can realistically be implemented.
