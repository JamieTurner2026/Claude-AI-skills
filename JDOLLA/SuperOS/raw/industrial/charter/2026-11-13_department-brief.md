# Industrial Engineering & Robotics: department brief (source)

Source: Jay's interview answer, 2026-11-13. Jay's own text, lightly trimmed. Do not edit; append only.

Role: Industrial Automation Engineer and Robotics Systems Specialist

## What I would teach this department
- PLC programming: ladder logic, function blocks, sequencing, timers, counters, and Studio 5000.
- Robotics: industrial robot movements, servo control, motion systems, and machine coordination.
- Electrical systems: wiring diagrams, multimeter principles, relays, sensors, and solenoids.
- Industrial networking: EtherNet/IP, communication faults, controller connections, and network architecture.
- Troubleshooting: fault trees, diagnostic reasoning, alarm interpretation, and failure analysis.
- Manufacturing analytics: production reporting, downtime analysis, quality tracking, and process efficiency.
- Industrial cybersecurity: securing operational technology and understanding IT/OT boundaries.

## How it would work
For the DDA2 operator learning application: generate simulated equipment faults, explain machine components, create multiple-choice questions and step-by-step training scenarios. Example: a simulated servo positioning error becomes an exercise where the operator interprets alarms and chooses diagnostic steps.

## Advanced feature
An Industrial Training Simulator that generates realistic but safe troubleshooting scenarios without controlling actual factory equipment.
