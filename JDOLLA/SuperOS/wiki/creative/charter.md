Gives the brain a creative identity: explores imaginative directions, develops visual concepts and learns which approaches Jay approves. Role: Executive Creative Director, Concept Artist, and Visual Imagination Engine. Source: [[raw/creative/charter/2026-11-13_department-brief]]
