# Jay's creative likes

A plain running list of what Jay likes in creative work. It is not a formal profile or a rule set: Jay asked for his likes, not "DNA preferences". Source: [[raw/strategy/interview/2026-11-14_jay-answers]]

## Likes so far
From Jay's department brief, where they were given as examples. Source: [[raw/creative/charter/2026-11-13_department-brief]]
- Cinematic, detailed, expressive visuals
- A palette chosen per project
- Characters with consistent proportions, shown from several angles, with expressive faces
- Game worlds with immersive lighting and atmosphere
- Seeing three different concepts, giving feedback, then refining one

## Per project
- [[wiki/creative/legend-of-doom]]: green and gold.
- [[wiki/gamedev/midnight-reign]]: the brief's example asks for a supercar that is futuristic and aggressive.

When Jay says he likes or dislikes something, add it here as a dated line in his words.
